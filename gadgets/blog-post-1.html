Best Wireless Earbuds Under RM200 in 2025

January 15, 2025 JOMKAKI Team 7 min read

Finding quality wireless earbuds that don't break the bank can be challenging. With dozens of options flooding the Malaysian market, we've tested and compared the best TRUE wireless earbuds you can get for under RM200 in 2025.

What to Look For in Budget Earbuds

Before diving into our picks, here are the key features that matter most when shopping for budget-friendly earbuds:

  • Sound quality: Look for earbuds with at least 10mm drivers for fuller bass and clearer mids
  • Battery life: Aim for at least 6 hours per charge with 24+ hours from the case
  • Bluetooth version: BT 5.2 or newer for stable connection and lower latency
  • Water resistance: IPX4 or higher — essential for Malaysia's weather and workouts
  • Comfort and fit: Multiple ear tip sizes ensure a secure, comfortable seal

1. QCY T13 ANC 2 — Best Overall (RM89)

The QCY T13 ANC 2 punches well above its price point. Active noise cancellation at this price is rare, and the 30-hour total battery life is impressive. The 10mm dynamic drivers deliver surprisingly balanced sound with decent bass response. App support lets you customize EQ settings.

2. Redmi Buds 5 — Best for Xiaomi Users (RM109)

Xiaomi's Redmi Buds 5 offer excellent integration with MIUI devices. Highlights include 46dB hybrid ANC, 40-hour total battery life, and dual-device connectivity. The 12.4mm drivers produce warm, engaging sound. A top choice if you're in the Xiaomi ecosystem.

3. Baseus Bowie MA10 — Best Bass (RM79)

If bass is your priority, the Baseus Bowie MA10 delivers thumping low-end with its custom-tuned 13mm drivers. The price is incredibly competitive, and you get Bluetooth 5.3, 48-hour battery life, and a comfortable semi-in-ear design. A solid pick for music lovers who enjoy EDM and hip-hop.

4. Samsung Galaxy Buds FE — Best Brand Name (RM199)

Right at the budget ceiling but worth every ringgit, Samsung's Galaxy Buds FE offer ANC, AKG-tuned sound, and seamless integration with Samsung Galaxy devices. The app provides extensive customization. Build quality and call quality are noticeably better than cheaper options.

5. Soundpeats Air4 Lite — Best for Calls (RM119)

With 6 microphones and AI-powered noise cancellation for calls, the Air4 Lite excels as a work-from-home companion. The aptX Lossless codec support delivers CD-quality audio, making these a standout for both music and calls.

"You don't need to spend a fortune for great wireless audio. These budget earbuds prove that quality sound is accessible to everyone."

Quick Comparison

All five picks offer solid sound quality, but here's how they differ:

  • Best value: Baseus Bowie MA10 (RM79)
  • Best ANC: Redmi Buds 5 (RM109)
  • Best for calls: Soundpeats Air4 Lite (RM119)
  • Best brand: Samsung Galaxy Buds FE (RM199)
  • Best overall: QCY T13 ANC 2 (RM89)
