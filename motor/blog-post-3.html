Top 10 Must-Have Motorcycle Accessories for 2025

February 5, 2025 JOMKAKI Team 6 min read

Whether you're a daily commuter navigating KL traffic or a weekend tourer exploring scenic routes, having the right accessories can transform your riding experience. Here are our top 10 must-have motorcycle accessories for 2025.

1. High-Quality Helmet with Anti-Fog Visor

Your helmet is your most important piece of gear. In 2025, look for helmets with integrated anti-fog visors — essential for Malaysia's sudden downpours. A DOT or ECE certified full-face helmet with proper ventilation will keep you safe and comfortable in our tropical heat.

2. LED Auxiliary Lights

Visibility is everything. Compact LED auxiliary lights increase your presence on the road, especially during dawn, dusk, and heavy rain. Modern options are waterproof, energy-efficient, and easy to install on any motorcycle.

3. Phone Mount with Wireless Charging

Navigation is essential for riders. A vibration-dampened phone mount with wireless charging keeps your device powered and protected. Look for mounts with IP67 waterproof rating for worry-free riding in any weather.

4. Performance Air Filter

Upgrade from your stock paper filter to a high-flow performance air filter. Better airflow means improved throttle response and marginally better fuel efficiency. Plus, reusable filters save money in the long run.

5. USB Charging Port

A handlebar-mounted USB charging port is a must for long rides. Keep your phone, action camera, or Bluetooth intercom charged on the go. Quick charge 3.0 models are now available at affordable prices.

6. Riding Gloves with Touchscreen Compatibility

Protect your hands with proper riding gloves that work with your phone's touchscreen. Look for gloves with knuckle protection, palm sliders, and breathable material suited for Malaysian weather.

7. Tank Bag or Tail Bag

A quality tank bag adds convenient storage for daily essentials. Magnetic or strap-mounted options are available for most motorcycles. Choose one with a clear top pocket for your phone to use as a GPS window.

8. Chain Maintenance Kit

Keep your chain in top condition with a dedicated maintenance kit. A good kit includes chain cleaner, lubricant, and a chain brush. Regular maintenance extends chain life and ensures smooth power delivery.

9. Crash Guards / Engine Guards

Protect your motorcycle's engine and fairings from low-speed drops and tip-overs. Quality crash guards can save thousands in repair costs and are especially valuable for newer riders still building confidence.

10. Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS)

Real-time tyre pressure monitoring prevents blowouts and improves fuel efficiency. Wireless TPMS sensors are now compact enough for motorcycle wheels and display readings on a handlebar-mounted screen.

"Investing in quality accessories isn't an expense — it's an investment in your safety and riding pleasure."
